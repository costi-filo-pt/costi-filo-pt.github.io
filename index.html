Why Clients Work With Costi

Coaching that fits busy lives instead of fighting them.

Many people do not need more random workouts. They need a clear plan, honest feedback, and someone who knows how to turn good intentions into weekly action. Costi's coaching is built around exactly that.

Whether the goal is getting leaner, moving without pain, building muscle, or feeling comfortable in the gym again, the process stays simple: assess, plan, coach, adapt, repeat.
